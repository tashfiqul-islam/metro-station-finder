## Summary

**Headline answers**

1. **Operator.** Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL, 100% government-owned company) operates and publishes. Its own letterhead (fare collection guidelines, dated 21 May 2023 in the site listing) names the **Road Transport and Highways Division** (Bangla: সড়ক পরিবহন ও মহাসড়ক বিভাগ), not the Bridges Division. The brief's "Bridges Division" premise is not supported by these documents. Primary.
2. **Fare authority.** The Dhaka Transport Coordination Authority (DTCA, under the Road Transport and Highways Division, Ministry of Road Transport and Bridges) fixed the fares by a notice dated **8 September 2022**, memo no. ৩৫.০২.০০০০.০১৬.৪৭.০০১.১৯-৩৭১, "following government direction", citing Metro Rail Act 2015 section 17 and Metro Rail Rules 2016 rule 22, and publishing the table under section 19 on www.dtca.gov.bd. Primary. The notice states no separate effective date; the first section opened 28 December 2022 (DMTCL FAQ).
3. **Fare rule.** Tk 5.00 per km; minimum Tk 20; maximum Tk 100 (Uttara North to Kamalapur, 20 km per the notice); 10% off when paying by smart card; free single trips for war-wounded freedom fighters to be arranged by DMTCL; special concession for people with special needs may be arranged. Primary.
4. **Official fare table.** A 17 x 17 station-to-station matrix (16 open stations plus Kamalapur), all values multiples of 10 from 20 to 100, symmetric, zero diagonal. Primary. Hosted on the DMTCL fare page (page content last updated 3 June 2026).
5. **Fare comparison: zero mismatches.** All 256 cells of the baseline 16 x 16 matrix equal the corresponding official cells (comparison script run 2026-11-05 against the dataset file; transcription of the scanned table checked for symmetry and diagonal). The official table additionally has the Kamalapur row/column, which the baseline lacks (not yet open).
6. **Fare history.** No revision of the standard fare found since the 2022 notice (Unverified as a negative; see Open questions). In 2024 the NBR said 15% VAT applies to metro tickets (Tk 20-100 would become Tk 23-115), but DMTCL did not raise fares and exemptions were extended (Secondary). A 25% concession for 65+ and persons with disabilities took effect 25 May 2026, superseding the 15% for persons with disabilities in the 2023 guideline (Secondary plus DMTCL sheet).
7. **Stations.** 16 stations are open (Uttara North to Motijheel, 20.10 km). Kamalapur (17th, 1.16 km extension, 21.26 km total) is under construction, not open. The baseline list and order is correct for the stations open on 2026-11-05. Two baseline names differ from other spellings (Bijoy Sharani, Shahbagh) and one official DMTCL page has typos (Framgate, Bijoy Sarawni).
8. **Launch dates (DMTCL FAQ, updated 17 Aug 2026).** Uttara North to Agargaon 28 December 2022; Agargaon to Motijheel 4 November 2023; construction inaugurated 26 June 2016. Kamalapur: 73.25% civil progress at 31 March 2026 (official); a 26 October 2026 news report says 85% overall and targets trial runs in February 2027 and commercial opening about July 2027 (Secondary). Wikipedia still says 1 January 2027 (Secondary, older).
9. **Coordinates.** Baseline coordinates are within 143 m of the OpenStreetMap station node and 118 m of the Wikidata coordinate for every station; **none exceeds 150 m**. Closest to the limit: Bijoy Sharani (143 m vs OSM). No official coordinate source exists. Secondary.
10. **Open data.** DMTCL publishes the fare table only as a scanned PDF image and the station and schedule lists as web tables; no API, open-data feed or machine-readable fare file found. No copyright or terms-of-use statement for the fare table found.

## 3. Fares

### Fare rule and tickets (Primary, S3 and S4)

- Rate Tk 5.00 per km; minimum Tk 20; maximum Tk 100 for the whole line (20 km, Uttara North to Kamalapur).
- Rounding. The notice states none for the standard table. Observed: every standard value is a multiple of 10 (20 to 100). Press reports Uttara to Agargaon (11.73 km) at Tk 60, consistent with 5 x 11.73 = 58.65 rounded to the nearest 10 (Inferred; per-station route distances are not published in the sources read, so the matrix cannot be reconstructed from distances alone, and none was needed because the table itself is published).
- Ticket types (DMTCL FAQ, 17 Aug 2026): Single Journey Ticket (SJT) and MRT Pass; DTCA Rapid Pass holders can also ride. SJT bought at ticket vending machine or ticket office, valid until midnight on the day of purchase (the English column reads 12:00 PM, the Bangla column says night 12:00; Bangla and the FAQ say midnight, so the English text is likely a slip, Inferred).
- MRT Pass (guideline of 21 May 2023, price may have changed): Tk 500 initial, of which Tk 200 refundable deposit and Tk 300 usable balance; 10-year validity; maximum stored value Tk 10,000; 10% fare discount. Top-up at vending machine, ticket office or excess fare office.
- Concessions: 10% for smart cards (notice item ঘ and guideline); war-wounded freedom fighters free single journey on showing ID at the ticket office; persons with special needs 15% on SJT (guideline, Department of Social Services ID); children under 3 feet travel free with a guardian.
- Senior citizens (65+) and persons with disabilities: 25% off single-journey tickets, effective 25 May 2026 (TBS, Secondary: https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/transport/fare-discounts-launched-elderly-disabled-passengers-metro-rail-1448521, URL confirmed by search 2026-11-05; the report says an event at Farmgate station launched it, IDs are required (National ID for seniors, the social-welfare disability card for disabled passengers), companions pay the full fare, and the 15% for persons with disabilities rose to 25%). Consistent with the DMTCL sheet (S5, Primary) signed 25.05.2026: fares are 25% off the standard fare rounded to a multiple of 5 with exact halves rounded down in its examples (22.5 gives 20, 37.5 gives 35, 52.5 gives 50, 67.5 gives 65); minimum Tk 15; Uttara North to Motijheel Tk 75; bought at ticket offices. The 15% in the 2023 guideline is therefore superseded for persons with disabilities; the senior 25% is new. The sheet is not a DTCA notice and its website listing date (09/10/2023) disagrees with its signatures (May 2026), so its listing date is unreliable.
- Overstay and penalties (guideline): paid-area time limits of 40 minutes (Uttara North to Agargaon) and 60 minutes (to Motijheel); overstay is charged double the maximum fare for the section; a person in the paid area without a ticket pays double the line's maximum fare. Not needed for the app.

**What "station location" means.** Neither OSM nor Wikidata states it. An OSM station node on an elevated viaduct station is normally placed at or near the station building (Inferred; not checked against imagery); it is not an entrance position. Entrances (stairs or lifts at street level) are mostly not mapped in the area (a single `railway=subway_entrance` node was returned in the whole bounding box, near Shahbagh) so entrance-level walking distance cannot be supported from these sources. No official entrance or platform data is published on the DMTCL pages read. For building and later entrance positions the most trustworthy available source is OSM, because positions can be inspected and corrected against imagery and the data can be re-queried with a timestamp; Wikidata copies it with a lag. A field survey or a DMTCL-provided list would outrank both (Unknown whether DMTCL would provide one).

## Method and limits

- Retrieval: raw HTTP GET of dmtcl.gov.bd pages and PDFs (a plain browser-style user agent), Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) and Wikidata `wbgetentities`; the English site is served by setting the cookie `lang=en`. Web search and fetch helper tools gave summaries for news, Wikipedia and DMTCL notices pages; those results are labelled Secondary and not used for numbers that matter except where stated.
- PDFs S3, S4 and S5 have no text layer; they were rendered to images with Windows PDF rendering and read visually. No OCR was used. Transcription risk is limited by the symmetric, diagonal and 256-cell baseline cross-check; page-1 rule text was read directly.
- Scripts (parsing the dataset array, symmetry check, Haversine) were run from the session scratchpad and are not committed; the arithmetic is reproducible from the matrix and coordinate tables above.
- The Bangla fare matrix reading: digits ০১২৩৪৫৬৭৮৯ map to 0123456789; ৪ and ৮ look like "8" and "b" in this typeface, the main misread risk, addressed by the baseline cross-check.
- Not done: no dataset, code, spec or glossary edit; no Plane writes; DTCA home page and Gazette rule text not exhaustively read; DMTCL Facebook, mobile apps and station signage not seen.
